The author describes this as a real wooden die rolling on a wooden table,
recorded with a Philips SBC MD110 microphone. Attribution is retained here
for provenance, although CC0 does not require attribution.

wooden-dice-roll-3.flac is the unmodified original recording.
../sfx/dice-roll.wav is derived from it by tools/audio/process-recordings.py:
70 Hz high-pass, mild transient compression, normalization to -3 dBFS,
2 ms fade-in, 15 ms fade-out, stereo PCM16 at 44.1 kHz.
The actual contact timings, length and pitch are preserved.
